Hi, I’m
Joshua Trujillo.

A mechanical engineering student who enjoys turning ideas into working systems.

My experience spans experimental research equipment, vehicle systems, and compact robotic mechanisms. I bring designs from CAD into the real world through prototyping, fabrication, assembly, and testing.

I’m especially interested in how individual parts come together as a complete system—from fit and alignment to instrumentation and control. My approach is hands-on: build, test, understand what can improve, and iterate.
